The Ladder
One parlay on a game day that has a good one, 2 or 3 legs from different games: NFL sides, totals, moneylines and prop unders, college totals and moneylines (never college spreads). Every leg beats its own price at the book that pays the most, and the ticket hits 15%+ of the time. A day without one is a pass: nothing posted, nothing tracked. (Before Nov 4 a pass day got a "light" ticket at ½u, the closest to fair; those are kept in the record, apart.) It locks 90 minutes before the first leg and is graded leg by leg. Each leg is also checked against its closing price, the last market price before kickoff: beating the close again and again is the surest early sign the picks are good, long before the win-loss record settles.
